Search surveillance clips by typing a sentence ("a person opens a car door"), while keeping the expensive AI calls to a minimum. Dataset: MEVA. Updated 4 Nov 2026, 15:45.

What we did, step by step

1

Choose the topic and dataset · 12–20 Sep

TriedSeveral ideas; some had no ground truth, were on Kaggle, or were already published (PlantCity).
DecidedMEVA (public, never used for sentence search). Proposal submitted 20 Sep.
2

Prepare the data · 28 Sep – 22 Oct

DidDownloaded ~70 GB of video, listed 10,678 activity clips, cut out the people in each frame. Two test setups: A = normal split, B = cameras never seen in training (harder).
Problem99.5 % of crops were wrong (annotation files had two formats) → fixed and re-done.
3

Pick the image model · 23 Oct · Result 01

TriedSigLIP vs SigLIP 2, with tight crop / wider crop / full frame.
FoundSigLIP 2 on the tight crop is best — about 2× better than the full frame.
DecidedUse it as the base for everything.
4

Baseline: search without any training · 27 Oct · Result 02

FoundScore (mAP) 0.179. Activities defined by movement (turning, opening doors) were at chance.
DecidedFine-tune the image model on our data.
5

Fine-tune the image model (LoRA) · 30 Oct · Result 03

Found0.179 → 0.313 (A) and 0.174 → 0.280 (B). Big improvement.
DecidedAdd a second stage: a small "student" that looks at the frame sequence, and an AI video model (VLM) as a teacher / final check.
6

Two-stage search with small 2B video models · 31 Oct · Result 04

FoundStudent works: correct clip ranked first for 53 % (A) / 52 % (B) of searches, with zero expensive AI calls. Score 0.476 / 0.442.
The 2B video models add little (≤ 3 points) and failed our quality check; teaching the student with them did not help.
DecidedYou answered 7 questions → a set of follow-up experiments (steps 7–10).
7

Follow-ups with the 2B models · 3 Nov · Result 05

QuestionAnswer
Run the AI model on every clip?No — slower and worse than the fine-tuned model.
Merge "turns left" and "turns right"?Yes — found well (0.31 → 0.57–0.62). Turning is seen; left vs right is the hard part.
Give the model 16 frames instead of 8?No improvement.
Quality check on all validation clips?2B still fails.
DecidedTry bigger video models on the office server. Your rule: check on the top 50 first, then on all clips; both must pass.
8

Other model families · 3 Nov

Gemma 4 E4BFailed the check (colleague's suggestion) → stopped and deleted.
Qwen2.5-VL3B and 7B (already on the server) failed → kept, because your nexa-vlm project uses them.
9

Bigger model: Qwen3-VL-8B · 3 Nov · office server

FoundMissed the check by a hair (0.0009 short). But as the last step on a short list it lifted "correct clip first" from 53 % to 59 % on A (a real gain); on B the gain was not certain.
DecidedTry the 32B model.
10

Biggest model: Qwen3-VL-32B · 3–4 Nov · office server

FoundFirst model to pass both checks: top 50 (+0.017 A, +0.037 B) and all validation clips (+0.018 A, +0.047 B). Bigger model = better, every time (2B → 8B → 32B). It is slow (about 25 s to answer 64 questions on one clip) because it does not fit in the GPU.
DidFull chain with it: labelled 41,266 training pairs → retrained the students → final two-stage numbers (4 Nov).
FoundTeaching the student with it still does not help (the student trained on human labels stays best).
As the last step on a short list of 10: "correct clip first" 53 % → 60 % (A), 52 % → 56 % (B). This is the first model whose gain is certain on both setups, including unseen cameras.
But it is not better than the 8B (difference under 1 point, could be chance), and each check takes ~13× longer (1.7 s vs 0.13 s per question).
ConclusionThe 8B is the best value on this hardware; the bigger model buys no extra accuracy. The small student stays the most useful step for its cost.

Words used on this page
  • mAP — how well all correct clips are ranked near the top (0 to 1, higher is better).
  • "Correct clip first" — share of searches where the very first result is right (S@1).
  • VLM — an AI model that watches the video and answers "does this show …? yes / no". Accurate-ish but expensive.
  • Student — a small, fast model trained to re-rank results; no expensive calls at search time.
  • Quality check (G1) — does adding the VLM to our fine-tuned model improve validation results by at least 0.02?
  • A / B — normal test split / test on cameras never seen in training.
