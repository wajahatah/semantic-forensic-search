## 2. The system before

  adaptation of the retrieval encoder by LoRA, (3) an accuracy-versus-cost frontier — and three
  hypotheses: adaptation improves first-stage recall; a distilled student keeps most of the teacher's

## 4. Report outline (page budget in brackets, 10 pages total)

**Cover + abstract** [0.4]. Title as in the proposal. Abstract ≤ 180 words with the headline numbers.

**1. Problem definition** [0.6] — from the proposal, then what changed: moment retrieval over a corpus
of *annotated activity instances* (the alert source), not free temporal localisation; Success@K added
as the forensic measure (Result 02 caveat); the research question restated: *how much of an exhaustive
VLM's retrieval quality can a cascade keep while cutting VLM calls by one to two orders of magnitude* —
and the answer that the measured 2B VLM-on-all is itself below the cheap stages, so the frontier was
measured against both.

**2. Dataset and query set** [0.8] — MEVA facts; what was actually used: 10,678 annotated instances with
downloaded video (~70 GB subset), Protocol A (official split) and B (unseen cameras), 22 query activities
(rare types excluded, D-012), the curated query set (66 frozen phrasings, 192 training captions, both
reviewed blind to results), the balance strategy as delivered (√-balanced sampling, macro metrics,
stratified queries), the constraint compliance (non-Kaggle, non-synthetic, CC BY 4.0).

**3. Literature review and what was missing** [1.5] — the seven works of the proposal kept and
corrected, plus nine added (§5), grouped: (a) contrastive encoders; (b) video and moment retrieval with
cost; (c) surveillance retrieval; (d) adaptation and distillation; (e) VLMs as rankers; (f) dataset and
detection tools. "What was missing", stated as three gaps the proposal set out to fill: no cost axis in
surveillance retrieval papers; MEVA never used for language retrieval (checked again 2026-11-07: TRECVID
ActEV only); no measurement of fixed-camera domain shift for a dual encoder.

**4. What we claimed and what we did** [0.8] — one table, proposal item → outcome → evidence:

| Proposal | Outcome | Where |
|---|---|---|
| LoRA domain adaptation improves first-stage recall | **Confirmed.** Q1 mAP 0.179 → 0.313 (A), 0.174 → 0.280 (B); Q2 S@1 0.27 → 0.39; 80 % of the gain transfers to unseen cameras | Result 03 |
| A distilled student keeps most of the VLM teacher's quality | **Not as claimed.** The 2B teacher fails the information gate (G1); distillation never beats training on the human labels (GT ≥ GT+KD ≫ KD), also from the 32B teacher. **What held instead:** a cheap temporal student trained on the labels is the biggest gain of all (Q2 S@1 0.39 → 0.53 at 0 VLM calls) | Results 04, 05 |
| Accuracy-vs-cost frontier | **Delivered**, with a different shape than hypothesised: the curve is flat-to-negative for the 2B VLM (VLM on all < LoRA alone), concave only after a good short list; the 8B/32B add +0.06 S@1 at 10 calls (significant), 32B ≈ 8B at 13× the time | Results 04, 05 |
| Cross-encoder as the final stage, invoked on few candidates | **Delivered** (VLM @5–10 on the student's list), measured for 2B, 8B, 32B | Results 04, 05 |
| Cost reported as invocations, tokens, latency, money | **Partly.** VLM calls and seconds per query; tokens and money not reported (local models, no API) | Result 04 |
| ChromaDB vector index | **Changed:** exact search (3,726 clips, 0.04 s); approximate search unnecessary at this size | spec-search-demo D2 |
| torch.compile, W&B, Optuna, ONNX/quantisation | **Not used:** small runs (≤ 2,700 steps), logs in CSV; grids fixed in the specs instead of HPO; nothing deployed to an edge device | Result 03 |
| Alert ingestion + query interface | **Delivered:** YOLO/ByteTrack alerts from any video, 5 s clips, the same embedding path; dashboard with Detect and Search tabs, cost line, confidence bands, 3D map | spec-video-alerts-dashboard |
| Release the query set | **Delivered** in the repository (`queries/`) | — |
| Image + text LoRA (added later) | **Measured, not adopted:** +0.047 on unseen cameras but −0.019 on seen ones; fails the text-drift guard | Result 06 |

**5. Method** [1.8] — the pipeline figure (F1); sampling and actor-region views; frozen SigLIP 2 and why
tight crops; LoRA (rank 16, 1.18 M parameters, label-aware sigmoid loss over the caption bank, early
stopping on validation); the gates G0/G1 and why they exist; the temporal student (per-frame LoRA +
frozen-context embeddings + 12 box-motion features, transformer, trained on GT / GT+KD / KD targets);
fusion of z-scores; VLM @k with the yes/no log-odds; the evaluation protocol (Q1 category, Q2 forensic
window, Success@K, macro over activities, bootstrap CIs) and the rule that nothing is chosen on the eval
split; alert ingestion and the dashboard in one paragraph.

**6. Experiments and results** [2.4] — in the order the work ran, each with its question, number and
reading: zero-shot view selection (Result 01); frozen baseline and what single frames cannot see
(Result 02); LoRA (Result 03, table with CIs, per-activity highlights); the cascade (Result 04: headline
table A/B, the cost curve F2, hypotheses H1–H5 verdicts); the VLM follow-ups (Result 05: 2B on all, 16
frames, Gemma 4, Qwen2.5-VL, 8B and 32B gates and cascade rows); image + text LoRA (Result 06); the
demo's parity with the evaluation. A hypotheses table closes the section (the proposal's three + H1–H5).

**7. System demonstration** [0.5] — the dashboard (F4), what the recorded video shows, measured timings
(Standard 0.6–0.8 s over 3,700 clips; Verified k = 10 ≈ 3–4 s; a 10 s upload searchable in 22 s).

**8. Limitations and threats to validity** [0.7] — scripted MEVA activities; one training seed (seed
noise ±0.017 measured in Result 06); small validation sets; turn direction unsolved (H5); the VLM gates
on 2B models and default prompts; footage unlike MEVA is a demonstration only; confidence bands are
relative; the 32B cost per call.

**9. Benefits, stakeholders** [0.4] — the proposal's table, shortened, with what the results support
(on-premise, 0-call search; the privacy point) and what they do not (no identification; human
verification).

**10. Conclusion and future work** [0.4] — the three findings; next: heading-relative motion features,
a prompt/representation study for the VLM gate, a second seed, text-side adaptation for unseen cameras.

**References** (not counted) — IEEE style, ~16 entries. **Appendix** (not counted, 1 page): the
reproducibility map (repository layout, commands per Result) and the full per-activity table.
